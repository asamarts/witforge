## Why

Third-wave adversarial audit of `W10-122` / `W10-123` and `v0.7.0..HEAD` uncovered concrete
correctness, security, performance, and `rustfmt`/`clippy` defects that should be fixed before
tagging `v0.8.0`:
- Exponential unmemoized recursion DoS and depth-32 nominal truncation in `rust.rs`
  `estimate_type_layout`, plus missing `#[allow(clippy::enum_variant_names)]` on generated enums.
- Case-insensitive `freeze` bypasses (staged/committed case-swap, committed `git rm`, default
  manifest) and `<symlink>/..` lexical collapse / repo-root empty pathspec.
- Symlinked/dangling `released` manifests ignored/followed in `lib.rs` and operational lockfile
  errors downgraded/swallowed in `workflow.rs`.
- `new-version` missing cross-root manifest guard, skipping manifest validation on `--major`,
  and silently ignoring missing `project.released`; trailing-separator symlink checks.
- `witforge-build` dangling `include!()` for empty standalone `.proto`, overly broad `pbjson`
  prefixes leaking imported descriptors, panic on package-less `.proto` under `pbjson`, and
  Unix backslash acceptance in `include_file`.
- `action/index.js` re-running `--format json` probe on signal-killed children, losing
  `failed-command` when SARIF flush throws, and unescaped multi-line `::error::`.
- `docs.rs` single-line ` *` prose corruption and aligned closing star-banner failures.
- `rustfmt` mismatches in `convert.rs`, `glue.rs`, `rust.rs`, and `rustfmt.rs` on long names,
  flags, single-dot args, `match` scrutinees, variant/enum arms, and deep nesting.
- Offline `fuzz/Cargo.lock` parsing in `ci/check-release.sh` and remaining `0.8.0` doc/spec drift.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] All verified third-wave audit defects across `freeze`, `lib`, `workflow`, CLI,
