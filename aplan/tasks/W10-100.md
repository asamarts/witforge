## Why

The post-second-audit 1.0 criteria require one real consumer to take a contract through a
minor evolution and regenerate it across two witforge releases. `W10-098` records the policy,
but no live task owns the evidence and `W10-002` therefore cannot derive this gate from the
task graph. This criterion is separate from independent adoption: the same-owner alint proof
crate may satisfy evolution evidence but cannot satisfy `W10-001`.

## Why it is untestable

The task verifies two changes and their CI results in a consumer repository. Reverting this
repository's evidence record cannot make the external consumer's compiled behavior fail.

## Decision

Use alint if it has a real contract minor to ship: one change evolves the contract and
regenerates with an earlier witforge release, and a later change moves to 0.7.0 and regenerates
again. If alint has no real evolution, wait for another real consumer; a synthetic fixture or
same-commit pin change is not evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] cite the consumer commit that introduces a real contract minor and its generated-output
      change under the first witforge release
- [ ] cite the later consumer commit that moves to 0.7.0 and regenerates the evolved contract
- [ ] both consumer commits have successful CI and exercise lock continuity, diff, generation,
      compiled generated code, and version conversion where the contract uses it
- [ ] record observed migration friction without generalizing beyond the exercised contract
- [ ] `release-1.0.md`, the release checklist, and the task evidence point to the same two
      consumer changes
