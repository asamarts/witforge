## Why

The MVP deliberately does not track declaration order; a reorder in an unreleased
version is caught only by review. The pre-golive option C adds a fingerprint
without per-entry churn.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] per-type and per-function order fingerprints are stored in an additive section
- [ ] `--against` catches reorders of common members
- [ ] additions/removals do not produce false reorder findings
- [ ] migration from v2 without fingerprints is defined
