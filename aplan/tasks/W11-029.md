## Why

Adoption material should follow a real independent integration and document the path that worked, rather than advertise a speculative framework.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] document the high-fit and anti-fit consumer profiles with links to normative subset/runtime boundaries
- [ ] walk one real multi-version contract through check, lock, Rust/Serde, protobuf, schema, glue, diff, and stale-output CI only where the adopter used those outputs
- [ ] show how application adapters retain ownership of Extism, Wasmtime, process framing, capabilities, limits, and failure policy
- [ ] explain how wit-bindgen or Wasmtime bindings complement rather than duplicate witforge output
- [ ] include the exact independent adoption evidence and avoid implying that examples or sibling projects satisfy W10-001
- [ ] remove or correct any prior adopter guidance contradicted by observed integration friction

## Why it is untestable

This task publishes documentation derived from an already tested external integration.
