## Why

Audit finding C4 (high, re-run in the first audit pass). A generator deletes files in its
output root that carry its ownership header and are not in the new plan. The Rust, proto,
and glue generators share one header (`// @generated by witforge; do not edit.` at
`emit/rust.rs:16`, `emit/proto.rs:20`, `emit/glue.rs:22`), so:

- `witforge rust wit --out gen` followed by `witforge glue wit --out gen …` deletes the Rust
  files, and the reverse;
- two WIT roots generated into one `--out` delete each other's files.

`emitters.md:36` and `docs/guide/generation.md:56` say the headers are distinct.
Reviewer-read: `resolve_paths` (`main.rs:359-365`) does not check that the `out` values of
one configuration differ.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] each generator writes a header that names it, and removes only files with its own
      header; files with the old shared header are handled by a documented rule
- [ ] a root that holds another generator's or another contract's files is refused or left
      intact
- [ ] a configuration whose `out` paths coincide or nest is a usage error
- [ ] tests cover both orders of two generators and two roots
