## Why

Audit finding D6. `release-1.0.md` gates 1.0 on independent adoption (`W10-001`, parked until
2027-01-15). The second audit shows that gate is not sufficient evidence on its own:

- the only consumer is alint's unpublished proof crate. Its contract (read 2026-11-18) is
  one version in one file with no dependencies, no enum, flags, or alias, and no one-letter
  or upper-case words, so it has never generated a conversion and exercises none of the
  defects in `W10-045` to `W10-067`;
- the first audit closed with "no remediation work remains" and the suite green while those
  defects were present, because the suite cannot see them (`W10-068`).

## Why it is untestable

This task records a release-policy decision.

## Decision

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-19: 1.0 requires all four, in addition to the
criteria `release-1.0.md` already lists and to the independent-adoption gate of
`W10-001`, which is unchanged.

1. Every task of the second audit is closed, or declined in writing in
   `release-1.0.md`.
2. The compile matrix of `W10-068` runs in CI.
3. One consumer has taken a contract through a minor evolution and has
   regenerated it across two witforge releases.
4. The surface decisions are implemented, not only made: `W10-093`, `W10-095`,
   `W10-070`, and `W10-059`.

## Edge cases

- Scope of the first criterion: the audit's tasks in this cut, `W10-038` to
  `W10-098`. `W11-038` and `W11-039` are ideas in the next cut. Default: record
  them in `release-1.0.md` as not required for 1.0, which is the written decline,
  unless the owner wants them in.
- A decline is a line in `release-1.0.md` with the reason, and the task is dropped
  with `aplan drop`, so the plan and the document agree.
- The third criterion does not replace independent adoption. alint's proof crate
  can satisfy it, by evolving its contract and moving its pin across two
  releases; the evidence is the two changes in its repository.
- The second criterion means the matrix is in the `needs` of `ci ok`.
- `W10-002` gets a blocker for each audit task that does not already block
  `W10-069`: `W10-071`, `W10-076`, and `W10-079` to `W10-097`.
- `ROADMAP.md` links to the criteria and does not copy them.
- The semver criterion of `release-1.0.md` has to allow the one release in which
  `W10-095` breaks the library interface on purpose.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `release-1.0.md` lists the four criteria above, each with the task or evidence that
      proves it, and says what happens to `W11-038` and `W11-039`
- [x] `W10-002` is blocked by the tasks those criteria name
