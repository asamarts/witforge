## Why

Reconcile is where append-only behavior, reservations, reintroduction refusal,
and protobuf number bounds meet. It must be transactional from the caller's view:
no write on failure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] next-number allocation skips 19000–19999 and refuses past the maximum
- [ ] removed members reserve and reintroduced reserved members are refused
- [ ] type/kind mismatches and reserved-number collisions are rejected; previous-baseline released checks live in W2-004
- [ ] no-write-on-error and atomic rendering are tested
