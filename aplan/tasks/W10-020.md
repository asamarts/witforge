## Why

`main` is ahead of the published 0.4.4 line. The unreleased work includes a generated
`convert.rs` whitespace fix, the W11-037 stale-lockfile fix, the W11-036 conversion-impact
notes and additive `diff` API, and documentation/CI updates. The public docs and examples
now describe `main` while `cargo install` still produces 0.4.4. The owner must choose whether
this is a 0.4.5 output-alignment patch or a 0.5.0 minor that also publishes the `diff`
improvements, then run the release checklist.

## Decision

Publish **0.5.0** on 2026-11-17. The minor carries the W11-037 stale-lockfile fix, the W11-036
conversion-impact notes and additive `diff` API, the generated `convert.rs` whitespace alignment,
and the documentation/CI updates. Wire numbers, protobuf output, JSON Schema output, and the
generated Rust type/trait surface are unchanged.
