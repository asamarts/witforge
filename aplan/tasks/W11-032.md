## Why

Document extraction/indexing is the leading self-owned product hypothesis because trusted Rust extractors, portable Wasm parsers, and process-based language/native tooling can share a bounded semantic contract. It still needs user validation before a repository or runtime exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] identify concrete search, RAG, repository-browser, documentation, and static-analysis users and their current extractor pain
- [ ] interview or obtain primary-source evidence from potential users rather than relying on internal preference
- [ ] compare existing extractor/indexing frameworks and state a non-generic differentiator
- [ ] design a bounded file/chunk request and chunks/headings/symbols/links/metadata/diagnostics result without silently adding streams or arbitrary maps
- [ ] test the contract shape with current witforge emitters in disposable scratch space and measure representative payload boundaries
- [ ] decide whether to create a separate project; if yes, create its own plan with end-to-end product acceptance criteria rather than implementing it inside witforge
- [ ] restate that the resulting same-maintainer project would not satisfy independent witforge adoption

## Why it is untestable

This is product-hypothesis validation. Any approved product implementation belongs to its own repository and tested plan.
