## Why

A plan and generated output that are not gated drift. Released directories must
not change; shared proto/glue output must keep its wire numbers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `aplan check`, `aplan verify`, and `aplan export-agents-md --check` gate the plan (CI ran
  them with a pinned aplan while the repository was private; they run locally until W5-006
  restores the CI gate)
- [x] released WIT directories and lockfiles are frozen from the manifest's history; per-version
  Rust modules are reproduced by `--check` and frozen by the consumer's CI
- [x] shared proto/glue output keeps its wire numbers: the generators require each minor's
  lockfile to continue the previous minor's, and `--check` gates drift
- [x] `wit/released` is append-only and validated
- [x] generated `--check` runs for every emitter
- [x] `buf breaking` is documented and exposed as an optional extra check

## Why it is untestable

This is CI orchestration and a pinned tool installation. The checks themselves
are shell and workflow behavior; the plan's own `aplan check` is the local gate,
and the workflow is reviewed rather than unit-tested.
