## Why

Eighth-wave pre-0.9.0 independent adversarial audit of `e04524c..8a7bfa3` and the local CLI test
gauntlet uncovered 17 concrete defects across the seven promoted `0.9.0` features:

- **High (6):**
  1. `diff::compare_git_base` (`W11-043`): single-version diffs copied sibling `released` into
     `<temp>/released` without sibling released directories, causing `ReleasedManifest::load` to
     fail with `WF9002`.
  2. `diff::compare_git_base` (`W11-043`): temp path redaction only ran on `Ok(...)`, leaking
     `<TMPDIR>/witforge-git-base-...` on `Err(...)` across stderr, JSON, and SARIF diagnostics.
  3. `docs/guide/reference.md` + `reference_doc_tests`: missing `0.9.0` CLI commands (`doc`,
     `deps`/`deps sync`), options (`--eq-hash`/`--no-eq-hash`, `--try-from`/`--no-try-from`,
     `--forbid-unstable`/`--allow-unstable`, `--git-base`, `--config`/`--no-config`/`--project`
     on `diff`), and `witforge.toml` keys (`rust.eq-hash`, `rust.try-from`, `doc.out`,
     `policy.diff-check`, `policy.forbid-unstable-in-release`), failing
     `reference_guide_documents_every_cli_command_option_and_config_key`.
  4. `completions.rs` (`W11-047`): bash, zsh, and fish generators walked only one subcommand
     level and omitted `deps sync` and its flags.
  5. `emit/convert.rs` (`W11-045`): `emit_try_from_impl` diverged from `rustfmt` (Editions 2021
     and 2024) when `trait_line.len() > 100` or `function.len() > 76`.
  6. `emit/rust.rs` (`W11-040`): `--serde` emitted `#[serde(with = "...")]` without `default` on
     record fields resolving to `Option<...>` containing `[T; N]` (`N > 32`), breaking missing-field
     JSON evolution parity with `N <= 32`.
- **Medium (5):**
  7. `emit/doc.rs` (`W11-044`): `plan_with_released` ignored explicit `--released` when calling
     `diff::compare_full` for adjacent version diffs.
  8. `workflow.rs` (`W11-047`): `collect_deps_snapshot` ignored empty directories, missing stale
     empty subdirs under `deps/`.
  9. `workflow.rs` (`W11-047`): `sync_deps` mutated earlier targets before validating/snapshotting
     later targets.
  10. `emit/rust.rs` (`W11-045`): `is_float_free_def`/`is_float_free_expr` lacked memoization
      (exponential traversal on diamond record/variant DAGs).
  11. `main.rs` (`W11-042`): `resolve_diff_policy_views` and `resolve_release_policy_forbid_unstable`
      swallowed malformed upward-discovered `witforge.toml` errors even when the config belonged to
      the target's enclosing project tree.
- **Minor / Docs (6):**
  12. `main.rs` (`W11-047`): `deps sync --check` fix-it hint dropped `--from`/`--released`/`--config`/`--project`.
  13. `emit/doc.rs` (`W11-044`): `index.md` summary line placed `decode:` inside breaking parens.
  14. `emit/doc.rs` (`W11-044`): `escape_table_cell` double-escaped `\|` into `\\|`.
  15. `emit/convert.rs` (`W11-040`): missing-required-field hint omitted `map<K, V>`.
  16. `workflow.rs` (`W11-047`): `copy_dir_no_symlinks` hardcoded `new-version` in error messages
      when invoked from `deps sync`.
  17. `AGENTS.md`, `crates/witforge/src/lib.rs`, `crates/witforge-cli/src/main.rs`: top-level
      command/emitter lists omitted `doc` and `deps sync`.
