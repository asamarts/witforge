## Why

Adversarial emitter audit verified false negatives in `clippy::type_complexity` and `clippy::large_enum_variant` (struct padding & alias depth), `rustfmt --check` failures in `fmt_let_stmt`, `push_let_else_err`, long option `if let`, and defaulted `convert.rs` fields, named-option wrapper collision bypasses and missing enum/method `reserved_prost` checks in `proto.rs`, tab/closing-asterisk/bullet-list issues in `docs.rs`, missing `--draft` override on `witforge generate`, and missing `no_std + serde` compile-matrix test coverage.
