## Why

Hosted CI on `e5cb7e5` caught two test-only lints under Rust 1.100 and `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"`:
- `clippy::assert_is_empty` on bare `assert!(...is_empty())` calls in `emit_doc.rs` and
  `subset_expansion.rs`.
- Unused `use alloc::vec;` import inside the generated `#![no_std]` scratch crate test in
  `subset_expansion.rs` failing when `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` is active.

- [ ] Bare `assert!(...is_empty())` calls carry diagnostic messages and unused `alloc::vec`
