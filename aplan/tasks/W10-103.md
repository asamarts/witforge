## Why

Audit findings F-02 and F-11:
1. `witforge freeze --config` ignores `project.<name>.released` and has no `--released` flag, silently checking `<root>/released`, reporting "nothing to freeze", and exiting 0 even on tampered releases.
2. `witforge freeze` does not validate `validate_retirement_order`, exiting 0 on non-prefix retirement (`v0.1` live, `v0.2 retired`) or all-retired manifests that `check` and generators reject.

## Decision

- Add semver-safe `freeze::check_root_with_manifest(wit_root, manifest: Option<&Path>, options)` (keeping `FreezeOptions` unchanged for minor semver compatibility).
- Add `--released <PATH>` to `witforge freeze` and honor `project.<name>.released` in `freeze --config`.
- Enforce retirement-order and non-empty-live rules in `freeze` and collect violations in `FreezeReport.violations`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `freeze::check_root_with_manifest` verifies a custom manifest path and rejects an explicitly supplied missing manifest
- [ ] `witforge freeze` accepts `--released <PATH>` and honors `project.<name>.released` from `witforge.toml`
- [ ] `witforge freeze` reports non-prefix retirement and all-retired manifests as violations (exit 1)
- [ ] Unit, CLI, and `ci/test-release-freeze.sh` tests cover custom manifests and retirement order
