## Why

A self-owned product cannot satisfy independent adoption, but a real domain product can expose repeated contract needs and attract unrelated adopters. Product work must follow evidence rather than manufacture a second internal consumer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] evaluate document extraction/indexing, artifact inspection/policy, configuration validation/migration, repository automation, and a standalone conformance runner
- [ ] research existing open products, target users, unmet jobs, distribution, extension ecosystems, and maintenance burden for each
- [ ] model the smallest useful contracts and classify them against the current subset, especially maps, recursion, streaming, large payloads, and async callbacks
- [ ] state why native Rust, Component Model, and process implementations are each needed rather than adding transports for demonstration
- [ ] rank opportunities by user value, differentiation, bounded technical fit, adopter pull, and risk of duplicating established tools
- [ ] preserve the decision that a self-owned product is not independent evidence for W10-001
- [ ] select one validation target or decline all; do not start a broad framework

## Why it is untestable

This task is market/product research and a design decision, not implementation.
