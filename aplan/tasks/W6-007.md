## Why

evolution policy requires new members to be defaulted so those conversions exist.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] old-to-new and new-to-old conversion rules are specified
- [ ] required additions are rejected unless defaultable
- [ ] generated conversions are tested across two versions
- [ ] removed/retyped members follow the evolution policy
