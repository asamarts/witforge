## Why

Rust consumers often want `TryFrom`/`From` trait impls for `convert.rs`/`glue` conversions and opt-in `Eq`/`Hash` derives on float-free contract types used as map keys or set elements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Evaluate orphan-rule constraints, frozen-version compatibility, and float-evolution hazards
- [ ] Implement strictly opt-in if consumer demand justifies the config surface, or record the decline and trigger
