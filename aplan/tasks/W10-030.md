## Why

The fuzz workspace is outside every automated gate: root `cargo fmt`/`cargo check` do not
see it, Dependabot has no `/fuzz` entry, and the security job does not scan its lockfile.
Its `wit_package` target also triggers Cargo's kebab-case warning, and the quality design
document does not mention the harness.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the fuzz target is renamed to kebab-case and `ci/README.md`/`fuzz/README.md` commands
      are updated
- [ ] Dependabot covers `/fuzz`
- [ ] stable CI checks run `cargo fmt --manifest-path fuzz/Cargo.toml --check` and
      `cargo check --manifest-path fuzz/Cargo.toml --locked`
- [ ] the fuzz lockfile's advisory/license/source coverage is either added to the security
      gate or explicitly recorded as a non-blocking decision
- [ ] `docs/design/quality.md` describes the fuzz harness and pinned nightly
- [ ] actionlint, shellcheck, the fuzz checks, and a short smoke run pass

## Why it is untestable

The deliverable is CI configuration and a nightly fuzz workspace, outside the configured
cargo test runners. Workflow linting, stable manifest checks, and the fuzz smoke run are the
applicable evidence.
