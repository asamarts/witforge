## Why

This succeeds `W5-002` ("Add aplan, release-freeze, and stale-output CI gates"), whose traced outcome no longer holds.

That task stays `done` and keeps its evidence: the work was finished, and what changed afterwards is the ground under it. This task is what now needs doing.

It traces the same section(s), which is what drifted:

- `docs/design/lockfile.md#2-version-directories-and-release-status`
- `docs/design/emitters.md#51-commands`

W5-002's CI job ran `aplan check`, `aplan verify`, `aplan export-agents-md --check
AGENTS.md`, and `aplan check-docs` with an aplan binary committed to the repository.
Before the repository went public, the history rewrite removed that binary from every
commit, and the job went with it: aplan has no published release CI could install.
Until one exists, the plan is checked locally, as `ci/release-checklist.md` says. W5-002's
release-freeze and stale-output gates still hold.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A `plan` job installs a pinned, published aplan release (no committed binary) and
      runs the four commands above on GitHub-hosted runners.
- [ ] `ci/README.md`, `aplan/README.md`, `ci/release-checklist.md`, and
      `docs/design/emitters.md` describe the gate as CI-run again.
