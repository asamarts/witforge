## Why

Fourth-wave independent adversarial audit before bootstrapping `witforge-build` and tagging
`v0.8.0` identified high-impact pre-release defects and irreversible SemVer traps:

- Irreversible SemVer trap: `RustOptions` and `GlueOptions` derive `Copy` while
  `#[non_exhaustive]` (preventing any future non-`Copy` field addition after `0.8.0`).
- Unconditional `usize::next_multiple_of` overflow panic in `rust.rs` `estimate_type_layout` on
  valid alignment-`>=2` record DAGs, plus non-canonical dependency `LayoutKey`, memo poisoning
  on depth/alias truncation, and cross-package alias cap in `resolves_to_unit_err_result`.
- `docs.rs` list-state tracking doctest escape (`cargo test --doc` failure) and continuation
  paragraph corruption, unvalidated `parse_external_link` destinations and `\https://...`
  breaking `rustdoc -D warnings`, asymmetric `/**** Hello. ****/` stripping, and `/// *` +
  `/// ***` thematic-break corruption.
- `rustfmt --check` mismatches (Editions 2021 & 2024) in `glue.rs` (`emit_encode_list_loop`,
  `emit_decode_list_loop`, `format_inline_result_encode`, `push_let_else_err`) and `convert.rs`
  (`record_body` `wrap_ok` field indentation), plus `list_element_in`/`option_payload_in`
  ignoring their dependency `context`.
- `freeze.rs` parsing historical directory (`tree`) or symlink (`120000`) objects at
  `<commit>:<manifest>`, `canonicalize_best_effort` failing on missing intermediate `..` paths,
  and unconditional POSIX `\` corruption.
- Inconsistent trailing-slash normalization on `--released` manifest paths and `check`/`inspect`
  symlink targets, plus `package_location` pointing nested/multi-file diagnostics at the wrong
  `package` line.
- `witforge-build` dropping generated types when `default_package_filename` is customized on
  standalone package-less `.proto`, silent overwrite on `.proto`/`.PROTO` case collision, and
  unconditional POSIX `\` replacement.
- `action/index.js` unretried response-body reads (`arrayBuffer`/`json`), Windows GNU `tar`
  `C:` drive-letter ambiguity, and undocumented `failed-command: "sarif"`.
- Missing `witforge-build` public API / versioning policy in `docs/design/public-api.md` &
  `docs/design/release-1.0.md`, unverified `ci/check-buf-history.sh` pre-bootstrap gate, and
  normative doc/conformance/fuzz/check-release/plan inconsistencies.
