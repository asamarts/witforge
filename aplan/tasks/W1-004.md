## Why

The model is the contract between `wit-parser` and the rest of the compiler. A
construct that converts but is not tested is a future silent divergence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] every primitive, record, variant, enum, flags, list, option, result, alias, and use alias is covered
- [x] docs, stability, and escaped identifiers are covered
- [x] canonical strings and member keys are asserted
- [x] tests fail with a useful diagnostic when a conversion is wrong
