## Why

The model is the contract between `wit-parser` and the rest of the compiler. A
construct that converts but is not tested is a future silent divergence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] every primitive, record, variant, enum, flags, list, option, result, alias, and use alias is covered
- [ ] docs, stability, and escaped identifiers are covered
- [ ] canonical strings and member keys are asserted
- [ ] tests fail with a useful diagnostic when a conversion is wrong
