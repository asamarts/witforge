## Why

Conduit has an accepted WIT Component Model direction alongside existing protobuf/gRPC plugins and explicitly plans Rust as an early component SDK. Its streaming connector semantics may remain outside witforge, while bounded processor/control contracts may fit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] pin the reviewed ADR, roadmap, current protobuf protocols, Go plugin interfaces, Wasm processor implementation, and Rust SDK plans
- [ ] determine implementation status of the accepted ADR and which contracts are expected to remain dual-transport
- [ ] identify exact duplicated source-of-truth, compatibility, or SDK-generation problems that witforge could solve
- [ ] classify streaming, acknowledgements, state/checkpoints, maps, services, resources, and bounded processor operations against witforge 0.4
- [ ] evaluate message/control sub-contracts separately from full connector streams
- [ ] account for the Go-first host and whether Rust/plain-Rust generation is actually valuable
- [ ] record smallest adoption slice, project-side and witforge-side gaps, and go/no-go recommendation
- [ ] record the canonical outreach path: a GitHub Discussion as directed by Conduit's contribution guide

## Why it is untestable

This is an external architecture and adoption-fit review.
