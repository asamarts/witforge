## Why

Candidate reviews and maintainer outreach need one evidence standard. Without it, the project can confuse architectural resemblance with an objective consumer benefit, send generic pitches, or count a same-maintainer demonstration as independent adoption.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] define the evidence required to claim a current use case, duplicated contract source, compatibility problem, or adoption blocker
- [ ] separate problems witforge solves now from upstream contract redesigns, missing witforge features, and runtime concerns outside witforge
- [ ] define a common fit matrix covering contract shape, execution paths, wire/schema needs, evolution pressure, migration cost, and smallest useful adoption slice
- [ ] record the canonical communication channel and contribution policy for every named candidate
- [ ] require project-specific, concise, human-reviewed outreach that links primary evidence and asks falsifiable questions
- [ ] define how a maintainer rejection, no demonstrated benefit, or 30-day no-response outcome is recorded without weakening W10-001
- [ ] state that examples, forks, and same-maintainer projects do not satisfy independent adoption

## Why it is untestable

This task defines a research and communication protocol. Reverting it cannot make compiled behavior fail.
