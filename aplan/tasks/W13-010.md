## Why

Rust consumers receive generated bidirectional `convert.rs` adapters across adjacent live contract versions (defaulting added `option`/`list`/`map` fields on minor upgrades, returning structured errors on removed required members or unknown cases, and omitting non-convertible types across major boundaries). Extending the same conversion semantics to TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, and C# gives every target language full contract-evolution parity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Share the version-pair convertibility analysis from `crates/witforge/src/emit/convert.rs` so all six languages apply identical minor/major conversion eligibility rules
- [ ] Emit deterministic bidirectional conversion modules for adjacent live version pairs in TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, and C#, including explicit omission notes across major boundaries
- [ ] Cover minor additions (defaulted `option`/`list`/`map`), removed required members, unknown enum/variant cases, and cross-major omission with golden and round-trip evolution tests across all five languages
