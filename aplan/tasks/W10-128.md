## Why

Seventh-wave independent adversarial audit of `44bc734..d9c7729` uncovered 15 concrete,
reproducible defects:

- `docs.rs` (`D1`–`D7`): `FirstLineFence`/`FirstLineBlockquote` with `ws_width` in `2..=4`
  desynced `list_stack` when splitting `marker_prefix` onto an empty line; fenced blocks inside
  lists did not break out on `col < lc`; `is_blockquote` hardcoded `col <= 3` and broke
  multi-line backtick spans inside `content_col >= 4` lists; `in_paragraph == true` used
  unpopped `list_stack` for `max_fence_indent`/`max_marker_indent` (both escaping doctests and
  synthesizing a doctest from harmless prose under `1000. item\n    - > hello`); CommonMark
  §4.6 HTML blocks inside multi-line backtick spans escaped as doctests; `parse_external_link`
  trimmed trailing space after `\` leaving an odd trailing backslash (`\)`); and
  `strip_blockquote` hardcoded 3 spaces for `\t` after `>`.
- `glue.rs`, `convert.rs`, `rustfmt.rs` (`F1`–`F4`): `fmt_single_arg_call_tail` off-by-one
  (`<` instead of `<=`); `fmt_struct_lit` exact-width-100 single-line struct lit in
  `push_let_else_err` (`GlueError::InvalidState { message }`); four unhoisted error struct
  literals diverging between Edition 2021 and Edition 2024 (`convert.rs` `variant_body` /
  `enum_body` `UnknownCase`, `glue.rs` empty-variant `UnknownCase` / `UnknownEnumValue`); and
  two unwrapped deep-nesting calls (`glue.rs` `target::from_proto(...)` and `convert.rs`
  payload-less `Result::Ok(())`/`Err(())`).
- `lib.rs` (`L1`): `inspect_manifest_path` allowed non-root directories and FIFOs through to
  `fs::read_to_string` (`WF9002` `EISDIR` or hang) instead of refusing non-regular files with
  `WF9004`.
- `ci/check-public-api-policy.sh` (`C1`–`C2`): shallow `0.8.0+` clones carrying `v0.7.0`
  skipped the fetch guidance; `done <<<"$(git ...)"` failed `SC2312` masked-return lint.
- `docs/guide/diagnostics.md` (`Doc1`): `WF1014` wrongly stated `@deprecated` paired with
  `@unstable` is an error (contradicting `model.rs`, `subset.rs`, `diagnostic.rs`, and
  `docs/design/model.md`).
