## Dropped

Dropped 2026-11-18 by the owner's decision: the fixes on `main` ship with 0.6.0 (`W10-069`), not
as a 0.5.1 patch release. The acceptance criteria below moved to `W10-069`, and `W10-039` to
`W10-043` now block `W10-069` directly.

## Why

51 commits since `v0.5.0` hold user-visible fixes (atomic generated writes, symlink refusal
in released directories, `diff` reversed-endpoint rejection and missing-lock guidance,
required-addition blocker notes, Action request timeouts) that the roadmap already describes
as released. A build of `main` still reports `witforge 0.5.0`, so users and diagnostics
cannot tell the two apart. Publishing them, together with the Action input fix (`W10-039`)
and the corrected documentation, makes the published tool match its documentation.

Whether these ship as 0.5.1 or are folded into 0.6.0 (`W10-069`) is the owner's decision; this
task records the recommendation to release them now.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the release follows `ci/release-checklist.md` with the corrected order from `W10-040`
- [ ] the changelog section lists every user-visible change since `v0.5.0`
- [ ] the tagged Action runs `check` and `diff` on a hosted runner
- [ ] alint's pin is moved or the reason for waiting is recorded

## Why it is untestable

A release is a publication step; the release workflow's gates are its evidence.
