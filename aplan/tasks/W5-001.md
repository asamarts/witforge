## Why

The first consumer is the proof. Generating from a synthetic fixture does not
prove the contract's real shapes, naming, or glue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] the alint/representative contract generates plain Rust, proto, and glue
- [x] generated Rust and glue compile in a test crate
- [x] proto compiles and round-trips through prost/pbjson
- [x] the full command sequence is deterministic and `--check` clean
- [x] any contract gap is recorded against the design
