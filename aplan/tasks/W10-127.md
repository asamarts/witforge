## Why

Sixth-wave independent adversarial audit after tagging `v0.8.0` (`44bc734`) uncovered concrete,
reproducible defects in `docs.rs`, `glue.rs`, `convert.rs`, `lib.rs`, CI verification scripts,
and design documentation:

- `docs.rs`: six remaining CommonMark doctest escapes (bare/empty list markers setting
  `in_paragraph = true`, empty list items not popping on blank lines, chained same-line nested
  list markers and first-line fenced code blocks, non-`1` ordered list markers interrupting a
  paragraph, `first_line_code` rewriting `  -` after a paragraph into a Setext heading underline,
  and hardcoded `col <= 3` in `is_atx_heading_or_thematic_break` plus Setext heading underlines),
  and Unicode `.trim()` in `parse_external_link`.
- `glue.rs` & `convert.rs`: Edition 2021 vs 2024 `rustfmt` divergence when
  `field_indent + 4 + v.len() == 100` in `push_let_else_err` and `ConversionError::MissingMember`,
  and missing deep-nesting call wrapping on payload-less `Result::Ok(())`/`Err(())` in
  `emit_decode_inline_result` and `Option::Some(decoded)` in `emit_decode_option_wrapper`.
- `lib.rs`: `--released /` fails with `WF9002` (`EISDIR`) instead of `WF9004` (`GUARD_REFUSED`).
- CI scripts: `ci/check-version-references.sh` did not check the published Action step display
  name and lacked `--is-ancestor` on `refs/tags/v${VERSION}` (same in `ci/semver-release-type.sh`);
  `ci/check-public-api-policy.sh` fell through to the legacy `0.6.0 -> 0.7.0` diff on tagless
  `0.8.0+` clones instead of instructing `git fetch --tags`.
- Documentation: `docs/design/conformance.md` falsely claimed `prefixItems` for Draft 2020-12;
  `docs/design/public-api.md` and `ci/README.md` still described `check-public-api-policy.sh` as
  freezing only `v0.7.0` instead of auto-advancing to the newest tagged release at or after `v0.7.0`.
