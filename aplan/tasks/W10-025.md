tasks are not lost between sessions. The audit covers the entire witforge surface, the
release state, test and coverage results, and the follow-up task map.

## Why it is untestable

This task records an audit. Reverting the document cannot make a configured cargo test fail;
the recorded commands, coverage output, probes, and linked tasks are the applicable evidence.
