## Why

Adversarial audit found that `witforge-build` generated wrong `include!` filenames for standalone contracts with uppercase or Rust-keyword package names (`HTTP:API`, `example:%type`, `example:extern`, `self:super`, `example:gen`), silently omitted `*.serde.rs` for Edition 2024 keyword `gen` under `pbjson`, lacked a `pbjson-build` customization hook (`ignore_unknown_fields()`), unconditionally rewrote `prost_include.rs`, omitted subdirectory `rerun-if-changed` directives and root-dir symlink checks, leaked `.google` prefixes to `pbjson-build`, and allowed path separators in `include_file`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `witforge-build` derives exact `prost-build` and `pbjson-build` filenames for uppercase and Rust-keyword packages (including Edition 2024 `gen`) in both standalone and `deps/` modes
- [x] `witforge-build` preserves indentation, splices only current-run packages, avoids unnecessary mtime churn, validates `include_file`, refuses symlinked `proto_dir`, emits subdir `rerun-if-changed`, filters `pbjson` prefixes to discovered protos, and exposes `with_pbjson_builder`
- [x] Integration tests verify uppercase/keyword packages, multi-major standalone, `pbjson` customization, symlink/error paths, and `#![no_std]` consumer builds
