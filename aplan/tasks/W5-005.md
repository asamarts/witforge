packaging, and CI must be ready for alint and public use.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] the self-hosted runner is retired safely

- [x] 0.1.0 is published to crates.io, tagged, and installs from the registry

## Why it is untestable

This is release preparation and runner transition. Its proof is the release
checklist, the published artifact, and CI status, not a unit test.
